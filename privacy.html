Privacy Policy

Weird But Real Tools is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Weird But Real".

The app uses read-only access (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) to read the channel's own statistics: views, likes, comments, watch time and subscriber count.

The data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. The app cannot upload, change or delete anything on the channel.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
